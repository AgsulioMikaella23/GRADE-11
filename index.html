SpongeBob and his job


SpongeBob SquarePants is an optimistic, cheerful, and hardworking sea sponge who lives in a pineapple in the underwater city of Bikini Bottom. Known for his infectious laugh, he works as an expert fry cook at the Krusty Krab and frequently goes on absurd adventures with his best friend, Patrick Star.
